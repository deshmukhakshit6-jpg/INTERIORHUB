GOT QUESTIONS?

Questions Worth Answering.

Find answers to common questions about our property, construction, interior design, and planning services.

01 What services does INTERIOR HUB offer?

INTERIOR HUB brings together property and plot sales, home construction, interior design, and planning consultation. Service availability depends on your project requirements and location.

02 Can I customise my home's interior design?

Yes. Interior design can be planned around your preferences, lifestyle, functional needs, and budget, subject to the agreed project scope.

03 How much does home construction cost?

The cost depends on the built-up area, design, material specifications, location, labour, and finishing requirements. A project-specific estimate is needed for accurate pricing.

04 Can you help me find a suitable plot?

We can help you explore suitable property options based on your preferred location, budget, plot size, and intended use, depending on available listings and service coverage.

05 Do you provide planning consultation before work begins?

Planning consultation can help clarify your requirements, space usage, design direction, budget considerations, and project scope before the next stage begins.

06 How do I get started with my project?

Start by defining your requirements, preferred location, approximate budget, and timeline. These details help establish the right direction for your project.
